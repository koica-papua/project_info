Papua MNCH Evaluation

Shared materials for the impact evaluation of the KOICA–UNICEF programme to improve maternal and newborn health in Papua, Indonesia (2025–2029). Maps, survey documents and progress material for the partner teams are posted here.

Programme

Regencies
Biak Numfor and Mamberamo Raya (Papua); Asmat and Mappi (South Papua); Fakfak and Pegunungan Arfak (West Papua); Raja Ampat (Southwest Papua)
Facilities
55 Puskesmas, of which 18 BEmONC; 9 CEmONC referral hospitals
Evaluation
Difference-in-differences against three terrain-matched comparison regencies, with survey rounds in 2027, 2028 and 2029
Partners
KOICA · UNICEF Indonesia · OHCC, Universitas Cenderawasih · Seoul National University expert team
