Performance dashboard

A two-track dashboard for the KOICA–UNICEF Papua MNCH programme. Track 1 tracks the quality of the evaluation survey as fieldwork happens. Track 2 shows programme progress against PDM targets by regency, for the Project Steering Committee and KOICA.

Placeholder. No data is shown yet. The panels below show the planned layout and indicators. Track 1 goes live with the baseline survey (fieldwork May–July 2027). Track 2 depends on the review of UNICEF data-sharing arrangements described below.

If Track 2 is not agreed

The same feedback is delivered as a monthly performance brief and a semi-annual integrated scorecard, distributed to the PSC and KOICA.

Implementation

  • Build: a lightweight setup using existing open-source dashboard tools, with no licence costs. The specific tools and access-rights arrangements will be confirmed after a feasibility review reported to KOICA within three months of project start.
  • Maintenance: hosting, updates and fixes are handled by the evaluation team for the duration of the contract.
  • Fallback outputs: the monthly brief and semi-annual scorecard are generated automatically alongside the dashboard, so feedback continues even if the dashboard cannot be maintained.
  • Handover: at least three months of parallel operation with UNICEF and counterpart staff before the end of the project, with user and administrator manuals included in the handover package.
  • Running costs after handover: use of existing UNICEF and counterpart IT infrastructure is considered first; annual running costs are estimated and reported to KOICA one year before the end of the project.
